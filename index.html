What is Gundam?

Gundam is a science-fiction franchise, created my Yoshiyuki Tomino, spanning multiple anime, manga, videogames, books, and merchandise.

It started in 1979 with the airing of the anime Mobile Suit Gundam, where in the far future, a boy named Amuro Ray is forced to pilot the Gundam, a type of mobile suit, or giant robot, to defend himself and those he holds dear. Ever since that anime aired, there's been multiple sequels, spin-offs, and other media made, but most of them keep the anti-war message of the original.

Tomino based Gundam on his experience of living during and after WWII Japan, where he experienced first-hand the meaningless of war, and how war hurts everyone. While his work involves giant robots punching each other, there's always the human suffering that war brings to all sides in Tomino's works and subsequent Gundam media: civilians, soldiers, allies, enemies, war hurts everyone.

Of note is the important distinction between what a mobile suit and a Gundam is, which people not familiar with the franchise might get confused with. The giant robots that are piloted by human inside of them, are called mobile suits. A Gundam is a name for specific mobile suits. In other words, a Gundam is a mobile suit, but not all mobile suits are a Gundam.
